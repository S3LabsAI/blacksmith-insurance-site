Holiday home protection tips for The Colony homeowners

The holidays in The Colony bring full calendars, packed driveways, and a steady stream of packages on the porch. They also bring a few risks that are easy to miss when you're busy.

From our office at Grandscape, I see the same handful of holiday home insurance claims every year across The Colony and the rest of North Texas, and most of them are preventable. Here's my short list for keeping your home safe through New Year's.

01

Leaving town? Make the house look lived in

  • Put lights on timers or smart plugs, and vary the schedule so it doesn't look automated.
  • Hold your mail and pause deliveries, or ask a neighbor to grab anything that shows up.
  • Have someone roll your trash cans out and back on pickup day.
  • Save the vacation photos for when you're home. Posting in real time tells people the house is empty.
  • Lock and double check the garage, side gates and sliding doors. A dowel in the slider track adds an easy extra layer.
  • Make sure cameras and doorbells are online and alerts reach your phone before you leave.
  • Leave a spare key with a trusted neighbor, not under the mat. Ask whether your local police department offers a vacation house watch.
02

Don't let a cold snap catch you off guard

North Texas weather can go from 70 degrees to a hard freeze overnight, and burst pipes are one of the most expensive claims we see.

55°F

The lowest you should set the thermostat while you're away. Never turn the heat off.

  • Insulate exposed pipes and outdoor spigots, disconnect garden hoses, and cover hose bibs.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is. If you'll be gone during a freeze, consider shutting it off and draining the lines.
  • Protect your sprinkler system's backflow preventer with an insulated cover.
  • When temperatures drop, open cabinet doors on exterior walls and let faucets drip.
03

Keep porch pirates guessing

  • Ship to your office or a secure pickup location, or require a signature on valuable items.
  • Consider a lockable delivery box.
  • Break down boxes from big gifts before putting them at the curb. A TV box advertises what's inside.
04

Celebrate safely indoors

  • Water a live tree daily and keep it at least three feet from fireplaces, heaters and vents.
  • Don't overload outlets, and turn off decorative lights before bed.
  • Never leave candles or the stovetop unattended. If you're frying a turkey, do it outside, well away from the house, with a fully thawed bird.
  • Test your smoke and carbon monoxide detectors.
05

A quick insurance check-up

  • Take a video walkthrough of your home, new gifts included, and save it to the cloud. It makes any claim much easier.
  • Check coverage on high-value gifts. Jewelry, watches and some electronics can exceed the limits in a standard homeowners policy and may need to be scheduled separately.
  • Planning an extended trip? Some policies have conditions for homes left unoccupied. Give us a call so there are no surprises.
  • Hosting a crowd? It's a good time to confirm your liability limits and whether an umbrella policy makes sense.
06

Holiday home protection FAQs

What temperature should I leave my thermostat at when I travel in Texas?

+

Keep it at 55°F or higher, even if the forecast looks mild. North Texas can drop below freezing overnight, and an unheated house is how pipes burst while you're away.

Does homeowners insurance cover a burst pipe while I'm out of town?

+

Many homeowners policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but coverage can depend on whether reasonable steps were taken, like keeping the heat on or shutting off the water, while the home was unoccupied. Check your policy or give us a call before you leave.

Does homeowners insurance cover packages stolen from my porch?

+

Personal property coverage on many homeowners and renters policies can apply to stolen items, but your deductible often costs more than a single package is worth. Check with the retailer or carrier first, since many will replace or refund a missing delivery.

Are expensive holiday gifts covered automatically?

+

Not always. Jewelry, watches and similar items often have lower coverage limits under a standard policy. If someone in your house unwraps something valuable this year, ask us whether it should be scheduled for its full value.
