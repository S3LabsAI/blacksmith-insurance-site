Questions about your information?

(972) 624-0239

This Privacy Policy describes how BlackSmith Agency (“BlackSmith,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), an Allstate insurance agency at 5752 Grandscape Blvd, Ste 225, The Colony, TX 75056, handles personal information collected through this website, our quote request form, and our other interactions with you. It is based on the Allstate Online Privacy Statement, because we write insurance through Allstate and your quote and policy information is also handled under Allstate’s privacy practices.

If you become an Allstate customer, you will also receive Allstate’s privacy notice for your policy in your policy documents. That notice and this Privacy Policy should be read together.

01

Information we collect

Personal information is information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you. The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • Contact details and identifiers. Your name, email address, phone number, and postal address. When you ask for a quote or buy a policy, this can also include your date of birth, driver’s license number, and similar identifiers needed to quote and issue coverage.
  • Insurance and commercial information. The coverage you’re interested in, details you tell us about your vehicles, property, business, employees, or drivers, your current coverage and renewal dates, and your claims and driving history.
  • Consent records. Whether you agreed to receive marketing emails from us, and when.
  • Website and device information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request, and the date and time of your visit.

Some information needed to quote or issue insurance, such as a driver’s license number or credit-based insurance information, is considered sensitive under certain laws. We use it only as reasonably necessary to provide the quote or service you asked for, to prevent fraud, and to meet our legal obligations.

02

How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you fill out our quote request form, call or email us, visit our office, or apply for and service a policy. We also receive information from Allstate and, as part of the quoting and application process, from third parties such as consumer reporting agencies, state motor vehicle departments, and insurance support organizations. If you give us information about other people, such as a spouse or another driver in your household, we use it only for the purpose you gave it to us.

03

How we use your information

  • To respond to you and provide quotes. We use the details you submit to contact you by phone or email about your request, prepare quotes, and help you choose coverage.
  • To provide and service your insurance. To set up, maintain, and change your policies, help with claims, and answer your questions.
  • To communicate with you. To send messages about your quote, policy, billing, renewals, and service.
  • To send marketing emails, only with your consent. If you check the consent box on our form, we may email you about insurance quotes, updates, and promotional offers. Giving this consent is voluntary and is not required to get a quote or buy insurance.
  • To run and protect our business and this website. To keep records, keep the site working and secure, prevent fraud and spam, and comply with the law and the requirements of insurance regulators.
04

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed for the purposes above, including with:

  • Allstate and its affiliated companies, which underwrite and issue the insurance we offer, to prepare quotes and to issue and service your policy. Allstate handles that information under its own privacy statement.
  • Service providers that work on our behalf, such as our website host, the online form service that receives quote requests (Jotform; see Jotform’s privacy policy), our customer-management system (AgencyZoom), and our email and phone providers. They may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Companies involved in your insurance, such as claims adjusters and insurance support organizations, when needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • Regulators, law enforcement, and others for legal reasons, when required by law or subpoena, or when we reasonably believe it’s necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud.
  • A successor business, if our agency is sold, merged, or transferred.

We may also share information in an aggregated or de-identified form that does not reasonably identify you.

05

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. Fonts on this site are delivered by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser loads them; see the Google Privacy Policy. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

06

Your choices

Marketing emails

You can withdraw your consent to marketing emails at any time. Call us at (972) 624-0239, write to us at the address below, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email. If you opt out, we may still contact you about your quote request, your policy, billing, or other service matters.

Access and correction

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct anything that is wrong. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before we respond, and we can’t change information provided by other companies, such as consumer reporting agencies; you would need to contact them directly.

Information held by Allstate

Allstate insurance customers can ask Allstate to access, review, and correct their recorded personal information by emailing customerprivacy@allstate.com or writing to Allstate Insurance Company, Customer Privacy Inquiries, P.O. Box 660598, Dallas, TX 75266-0598. Include only your name, state of residence, and the nature of your request. To ask that Allstate not share your information with its affiliates for marketing, call Allstate at 1-800-856-2518. Residents of states with consumer privacy laws can submit access, deletion, and correction requests to Allstate as described in the Allstate Privacy Center, or by calling 1-800-624-4419.

Non-discrimination

We won’t treat you differently for exercising any of the privacy rights described in this policy.

07

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, and for as long as insurance laws and regulations require us to keep agency records.

08

Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information, and we limit access to the people who need it to do their jobs. No website, database, or system is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security. Please don’t send sensitive information such as Social Security or driver’s license numbers through our website form or by regular email; call us instead.

09

Children

This website is not intended for children. We do not market to children under 13 or knowingly collect their personal information, and you must be 18 or older to give consent through our quote form.

10

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, including Allstate’s. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we aren’t responsible for them. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
